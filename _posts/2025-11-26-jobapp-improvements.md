# Intro

Employers want to see impact within your past experiences, showing that you are a useful human being that can add to their bottomline. 

Currently in between college and my first industry job I have decided that I can impact or improve my performance in my own job search. 

# Strategies

We will start with a simple question: What are the pain points of the job search/ application? 

We can split this problem into two seperate groups: job search and the application process. Where the Job search aspect is finding jobs that align with your skills, interest, experience level. Application process is the process of securing the job via editing resume to pass the ATS system, writing a cover letter, filling out the online application, interview prep, interviewing, etc.

What tools/ strategies could we use for each?

**Job Search**:

- narrow scope (industry/ role) that you are applying for, get more specific in the tools that you are expected to use. 
- market research on what skills or key words are important 
- choose different job sites, employeers jobboard, webscrape, and analyze.


**Application Process**:

- auto fill via a google chrome extension?
- automatically create/ edit your resume via google chrome extension
- automatically create a cover letter based on job description and experience
- generate possible interview questions and topics to prep for
- automatic research on culture and fit

We want an automated report on the position including:

- company culture
- possible pay for the position
- role summary
- fit? 
- skills
- resume
- cover letter 
- possible interview questions
