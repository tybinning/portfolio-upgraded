# Introduction

In this project we will be predicting real estate property prices 5 years in the future assisting in our with our investment strategy and aiding in choosing the right property.

# Data Engineering

We will need to collect a variety data from different sources.

- Macro - Economic Data
- Local Economic Data
- Spatial Data
- Crime Rate Data/ Census Data for Targeted Area
- Consumer Sentiment
- Local Price Values
