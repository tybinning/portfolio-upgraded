# TF-IDF
Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) is a value assigned to each word or token within a corpus. This metric is calculated by taking the frequency of a term within a specific document and dividing that by the inverse of the frequency of how many documents have that term. I know that was a mouthful but the main purpose is to identify how unique a certain word or token is to a specified document.
